Year at a Glance

Pakistan Fruit Calendar

A quick guide to what's freshest and when — so you always order at the right time.

🌸 March – May (Spring)

Strawberries, guavas, lemons, and the early arrival of mangoes from Sindh. Markets are colourful and prices are fair.

☀️ June – August (Summer)

The king of seasons — Sindhri, Chaunsa, and Langra mangoes dominate. Watermelons, peaches, and lychees are at their best.

🍂 September – November (Autumn)

Grapes, pomegranates, and pears come into abundance. The last of the mangoes and the first Balochistan apples appear.

❄️ December – February (Winter)

Citrus season! Kinno oranges, kinnows, and sweet limes flood the markets. Apples remain in cold storage through the season.
