Our Story

From a Single Cart to Karachi's Favourite Fruit Delivery

Maru Fruits was founded in 2012 by Ahmed Maru with a simple idea: bring the freshest fruits from Pakistan's farms directly to families in Karachi — no middlemen, no waiting, no compromise on quality.

What started as a small pushcart in Clifton has grown into a full-service delivery operation, serving over 10,000 households across Karachi every week. We work directly with farmers in Sindh, Punjab, and Balochistan to ensure every fruit arrives at its peak flavour and freshness.

Our promise has never changed: farm-fresh fruits, honest prices, and delivery you can count on.

What We Stand For

Our Mission & Values

Every decision we make comes back to one question: is it good for our customers and good for Pakistan's farmers?

🌱

Farm to Door

We source directly from over 40 farms across Pakistan, cutting out every unnecessary step between harvest and your table.

🔬

Quality Control

Every batch is inspected at our Karachi warehouse — we hand-sort, grade, and reject anything below our freshness standard.

🤝

Farmer Partnerships

We pay farmers fair prices and build long-term relationships. Happy farmers grow better fruit — it's that simple.

🚀

Reliable Delivery

Our fleet of refrigerated vehicles covers all of Karachi with same-day delivery for orders placed before 2 PM.

♻️

Eco Packaging

We use biodegradable and recyclable packaging to minimise our environmental footprint across every delivery.

💬

Customer First

Not happy with your order? We replace it or refund it — no questions asked. Your trust is our biggest asset.
